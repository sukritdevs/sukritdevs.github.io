SE is standard error. Bold marks the best result in each column, not counting your own selection. Group reliability is 1 minus the average of 1 / (1 + information) across people drawn from a standard normal, so it runs from 0 to 1 and higher is better.

Rank and cut

Scores every item by how much information it gives across all trait levels (the area under its curve), sorts them, and keeps the top ones until the target length is reached. It is quick and easy to explain, but it never looks at domains or at what the scale is for, so it keeps the same items whatever the purpose.

Search

Starts from all 20 items and removes one at a time, each time the item whose removal leaves the best score for the purpose you chose. One rule stays fixed: every domain keeps at least two items. This toy uses a simple greedy search so it runs instantly. The real app also offers ant colony, genetic and annealing searches.
